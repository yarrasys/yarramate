# Reconciliation reports findings without remediation

Status: accepted

YarraMate Core may aggregate declared evidence overlays into a deterministic
workspace reconciliation report. Confirmed observations are summarized;
contradicted, unknown, and unobserved targets become review findings with
their provider provenance. The report does not propose replacement claims,
mutate native documents, define CI policy, or authorize remediation. This
gives agent harnesses a stable review surface while preserving the authority
boundary between evidence, architectural intent, and Git acceptance.
